When to Visit Goa

November to February (Peak Season): Perfect beach weather. Mild temperatures (20°C - 30°C), cool sea breezes, and active water sports.

March to May (Summer Season): Warm temperatures. Excellent for budget travelers wanting cheaper luxury resorts and peaceful beaches.

June to October (Monsoon Magic): Lush tropical greens, waterfalls in full glory (like Dudhsagar), and a romantic quiet ambiance.

Popular Goa Sightseeing

  • Baga & Calangute Beach: The hub of nightlife, street markets, and parasailing.
  • Basilica of Bom Jesus: A UNESCO world heritage church containing relics of St. Francis Xavier.
  • Fontainhas (Latin Quarter): Pastel-colored Portuguese homes and beautiful cafes in Panaji.
  • Dudhsagar Waterfalls: A massive four-tiered waterfall cascading down the Western Ghats.
  • Cabo de Rama Fort: Ancient fort in South Goa offering dramatic sunset cliff views.

Local Travel Tips

  • Pre-Book Taxis: Public transport is sparse in Goa. Pre-book taxi fleets like Seashine to avoid high on-the-spot rates.
  • Dress Light: Cotton clothing, sunglasses, and high SPF sunscreen are recommended throughout the year.
  • Scooter Rentals: Only rent vehicles carrying commercial yellow-on-black number plates. Remember to wear a helmet.
  • Cash is Useful: While UPI works, network issues near remote beaches make carrying some hard cash a smart idea.

Adventure Safety Guidelines

  • Life Jackets: Mandatory for all water sports, boat cruises, and waterfall swims.
  • Follow Guides: Pay attention to the instructions given by licensed PADI scuba divers and jet ski operators.
  • No Swimming Under Influence: Never swim in the sea or rivers after consuming alcohol. Strong currents exist.
  • Red Flags: Obey the beach flags. Red flags indicate strict "no swimming" warning zones.
